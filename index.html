About Me

Where Art
Meets Well-being

I'm Kate Berk, a community builder, arts-in-health practitioner, and person-centered coach based in Cleveland, Ohio. In fourth grade, a public school outreach program called Minds in Motion transformed the way I saw myself and the world around me. It taught me how to listen to my body and pursue goals with purpose, but more importantly, how to turn that discipline into connection and community. As much as I loved to dance, I always knew that its impact was far more expansive than I could comprehend.

"A dance outreach program changed my life in fourth grade, and shaped the commitment I now bring to every community I work with."

I earned my BS in Dance–Arts Administration from Butler University and spent the next decade as a Principal Dancer with Ohio Contemporary Ballet. During those years I performed domestically and internationally, served as the peer-elected Dancer Representative to the board of directors, and facilitated several outreach programs with strong well-being outcomes.

I now pursue an MA in Arts in Medicine with a Social Prescribing focus at the University of Florida, where I serve as a Research Assistant at the Center for Arts in Medicine Interdisciplinary Lab. I founded the Ohio Social Prescribing Collaborative, sit on the editorial board for the Perspectives on Arts in Health Journal, and serve as SAFE Project Coordinator at MetroHealth's Center for Arts in Health, where I oversee an arts-based curriculum supporting student resilience across seven grade levels in the Cleveland Metropolitan School District. I engage with working groups for both NOAH and Social Prescribing USA, and recently completed the National Board of Health and Wellness Coach certification through the Community as Medicine program at Open Source Wellness. I hope to use everything I have learned and lived to help people, and the communities around them, thrive.

Whether I'm facilitating a statewide coalition meeting, coaching a pre-professional ballet student to strengthen her technique, or leading a group of older adults connecting with movement over Zoom, I meet people exactly where they are. I work with dancers and non-dancers alike; the goal is never performance for its own sake, but rather the kind of embodied self-awareness and genuine connection that movement makes possible. I also choreograph around the Cleveland area for community-building projects, including the Creative Healthy Aging collaboration between Summa Health and Akron Civic Theater.

Outside of work, I love traveling, taking on new physical challenges, and enjoying chocolate, coffee, and vegetarian delicacies whenever possible. I spend as much time as I can with my husband and our dog, and more than anything, I love building meaningful connections with the people I meet along the way. I hope to do the same with you!

Where Movement
Becomes Medicine

Arts in Motion Wellness is my private practice at the intersection of movement, arts, and community health. Whether you're an individual seeking joyful embodied wellness, a healthcare provider curious about social prescribing, or an organization looking to bring arts-based programming to your community, I'd love to connect.

Private & Small Group Lessons

Virtual and in-person movement instruction tailored to your goals, ballet technique, joyful movement for wellness, alignment work, or dance as a tool for emotional regulation and self-expression. All ages and abilities welcome.

Social Prescribing Consulting

Consulting for healthcare systems, community health organizations, and nonprofits interested in integrating arts-based social prescribing into their practice, drawing on graduate research, clinical training, and a decade of community partnership work.

Arts in Health Program Design

Collaborative design and facilitation of arts-in-health programming for hospitals, senior communities, schools, and nonprofits. Trauma-informed, evidence-informed, and built around your community's specific strengths and needs.
